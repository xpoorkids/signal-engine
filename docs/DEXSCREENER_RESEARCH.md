# DEX Screener Research

The DEX Screener adapter supports token-pair lookup, pair lookup, latest token profiles, latest boosts, top boosts, and paid-order lookup.

Most DEX Screener fields are current or recent context. They are cached for provenance and prospective archives, but current liquidity, current volume, boosts, paid profiles, and social links are not used as launch-time historical features.

Pair creation time may help lifecycle anchoring when validated against another source.
