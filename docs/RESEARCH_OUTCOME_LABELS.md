Outcome labels are versioned under `memecoin-outcome-labels-v1`.

- `revival_runner`
- `community_takeover_runner`
- `slow_grind_winner`
- `chart_only_winner`
- `execution_failed_winner`

## Metrics

The outcome builder tracks maximum favorable excursion, maximum adverse excursion, target-before-stop ordering, executable net return when available, liquidity failure, route failure, disappearance, creator or insider selling, catalyst invalidation, and retained percentage of peak.

Right-censored rows remain unresolved. They are not counted as failures.
